Clippers R Us - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Clippers R Us is a desktop tool that edits short gaming highlight videos and posts them to the user's own TikTok account after the user approves each video.

What we access from TikTok:

  • Basic profile info (display name), to show which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos you approve to your account.

How it's stored: Login tokens and videos are stored only on the user's own computer. We do not run servers that collect this data, and we do not sell or share any data with third parties.

What we don't do: We don't read your messages, followers, or private data, and we never post anything without your approval.

Deleting your data: Disconnect the account in the app, or remove "Clippers R Us" under TikTok Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions. Deleting the app folder removes all stored data.

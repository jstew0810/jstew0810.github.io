Clippers R Us - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Clippers R Us is a desktop tool that edits short gaming highlight videos and posts them to the user's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts after the user approves each video.

What we access

TikTok: basic profile info (display name), to show which account is connected, and permission to upload and publish videos you approve.

YouTube: Clippers R Us uses YouTube API Services. With your permission it reads your channel name (to show which channel is connected), uploads videos you approve, and reads view counts of videos it uploaded so you can see how they perform.

Instagram: your account's username and ID, to show which account is connected, and permission to publish Reels you approve.

How data is used, stored and shared

Login tokens, videos and view counts are stored only on the user's own computer and are used only to post your approved videos and show their stats in the app. We do not run servers that collect this data, we do not use cookies or tracking, and we do not sell or share any data with third parties.

What we don't do

We don't read your messages, followers or other private data, and we never post anything without your approval.

Third-party policies

By connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. TikTok and Instagram data is also covered by the TikTok Privacy Policy and the Instagram Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data / removing access

  • Disconnect any account in the app (Accounts page), which deletes its stored login token. Deleting the app folder removes all stored data.
  • Google / YouTube: remove "Clippers R Us" at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.
  • Instagram: Settings > Website permissions > Apps and websites.
